Sample Study Notes

English: What is a noun?

A noun is a word used to name a person, place, animal, thing or idea.

Examples: teacher, Ghana, dog, book and honesty.

Mathematics: Percentage

A percentage expresses a number as a fraction of 100.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100%.

Example: 20 out of 50 is (20 ÷ 50) × 100% = 40%.

Science: Components of air

Air is a mixture of gases. It contains mainly nitrogen and oxygen, together with argon, carbon dioxide, water vapour and other gases.

Oxygen supports respiration and combustion. Carbon dioxide is used by green plants during photosynthesis.

Literature: What is a theme?

A theme is a central idea or message explored in a literary work.

Examples include love, betrayal, courage, friendship and justice.

ICT: What is a computer network?

A computer network is a group of connected devices that can communicate and share information or resources.

Examples include a Local Area Network (LAN) and a Wide Area Network (WAN).

Social Studies: Citizenship

Citizenship is the legal membership of a country, involving rights and responsibilities recognised by that country.

Economics: What is demand?

Demand is the quantity of a good or service consumers are willing and able to buy at different prices during a given period.
